Origin & Philosophy

The Story of Aary's IDE

Why we built one universal agent instead of another disconnected chatbot.

For years, developers were promised that artificial intelligence would transform software engineering. But what we received was a fragmented constellation of disconnected tools: chat windows trapped in browser tabs that had no idea what was running on our machines, code editors that only completed syntax, and mobile apps that forgot what we discussed the moment we closed our phones.

When you left your desk, your work stopped. If you wanted to test your application, you had to manually run the terminal commands. If you wanted to check on a build while traveling, you had to SSH through clumsy mobile terminals. And if you switched between AI models — from OpenAI to Claude or local Ollama — every scrap of memory, context, and project history vanished into thin air.

"The future isn't a chatbot that suggests code. The future is an autonomous partner that understands the outcome, remembers the context, controls the tools, verifies the result, and works seamlessly across your Mac, your phone, and your cloud workspaces."

Aary's IDE was born from this singular mission. We discarded the convention of tying agent memory to an AI provider. We decoupled the execution engine from the interface so that the exact same autonomous brain runs whether you trigger it from Spotlight on your Mac, send a voice note through WhatsApp while on the road, or open the dashboard on your Android tablet.

With real verification loops — where an action is only marked complete after actual disk inspection, test executions, and git pushes — Aary's IDE transforms AI from a speculative assistant into a dependable, production-grade engineer.

Easy Setup

Step-by-Step Installation & Setup

Get up and running on macOS or Android in under two minutes.

🍏

macOS Setup

Apple Silicon & Intel
  1. Download Package: Click "Download for macOS" to get the .zip bundle.
  2. Extract & Install: Unzip and drag AarysIDE.app into your /Applications folder.
  3. Grant Permissions: In System Settings → Privacy & Security, allow Accessibility and Screen Recording for Computer Use.
  4. Launch: Open from Applications or run ./run.command to start the Central Agent Runtime.
🤖

Android Setup

Phones & Tablets
  1. Download APK: Click "Download Universal APK" on your Android device.
  2. Allow Unknown Sources: When prompted by Chrome or Files, toggle "Allow from this source".
  3. Install App: Tap Install and open Aary's IDE.
  4. Pair Device: Grant Microphone & Notification permissions; tasks will automatically synchronize with your Mac workstation.
🌐

Vercel Web Deploy

1-Click Production Hosting
  1. Connect GitHub: Import your aarys-ide-website repo on Vercel.
  2. Build Settings: Root directory ./, Framework: Other / Static HTML.
  3. Deploy: Click Deploy — Vercel reads vercel.json and immediately hosts your live site and download packages!

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Aary's IDE work offline?

Yes! With Ollama or custom local OpenAI-compatible models connected, the central agent runtime, persistent memory, and filesystem tools operate completely locally without an internet connection.

How does the WhatsApp gateway work?

Aary's IDE connects to the official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API. You can send voice notes or text commands from your phone, which are transcribed, planned, and executed on your host workstation or remote workspace.

Can I continue tasks across Mac and Android?

Yes. The shared workspace and device registry syncs active tasks, memory, and conversation history across all paired devices in real-time.
